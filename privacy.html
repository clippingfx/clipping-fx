Clipping FX is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer.

1. Data we access

When you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token and your basic profile information (display name) through TikTok Login Kit. It only uses them to send the videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

2. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only on your computer, in the Windows Credential Manager. Your videos and clips stay on your computer. The developer has no server and does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

3. Third parties

Videos you choose to send are uploaded directly from your computer to TikTok, under TikTok's own privacy policy. If you enable the optional AI features, the text transcription of a clip is sent to the AI provider you configure (for example Google Gemini) to write titles and captions.

4. Deleting your data

Click "Déconnecter" in the app settings to delete the TikTok token from your computer, or revoke access in your TikTok account settings. Uninstalling the app removes all its local data.
