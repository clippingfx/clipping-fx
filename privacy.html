Clipping FX is a desktop application for Windows. Your videos and clips are processed on your own computer.

1. Data we access

TikTok: when you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token and your basic profile information (display name) through TikTok Login Kit. It only uses them to send the videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

YouTube / Google: when you connect YouTube, the app asks for the youtube.upload permission and your Google account email (to show which account is connected). It only uses this access to upload the clips you explicitly choose to your own YouTube channel, with the title, description and tags shown in the app. It never reads, edits or deletes your existing videos, and never uploads anything without your click.

2. Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on your computer, in the Windows Credential Manager. Your videos and clips stay on your computer until you choose to upload them. To keep the app's API keys secret, token requests and AI requests go through the developer's server (Supabase); this server does not store your tokens, videos or clips.

Your Clipping FX account (email address and subscription end date) is stored on Supabase to give you access to the app.

3. Third parties

Videos you choose to send are uploaded directly from your computer to TikTok or YouTube, under their own privacy policies (TikTok, Google). Clipping FX uses YouTube API Services: by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. For AI features, the text transcription of a clip is sent to Google Gemini to write titles and captions.

4. Google user data

Clipping FX's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

5. Deleting your data

Click "Déconnecter" in the app settings to delete the TikTok or YouTube token from your computer, or revoke access in your TikTok settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes all its local data. To delete your Clipping FX account, email clippingfx.app@gmail.com.
