Turn live streams into vertical clips

Clipping FX is a desktop application for Windows that helps content creators turn long videos and live streams into short vertical clips (9:16) for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Features

  • Finds the best moments of a video or live stream, including clips created by viewers on Twitch.
  • Edits them automatically in vertical format: streamer camera, gameplay, titles and subtitles.
  • Writes titles, captions and hashtags with AI.
  • Sends the clips you choose to your own TikTok account as drafts (Content Posting API, inbox upload). You then add your caption and publish from the TikTok app. Nothing is ever published automatically.
